# BLACKOUT CLAUSE — Map Concepts

## Design Pillars

- Every map should visibly favor at least two roles and punish at least one, so map choice becomes part of team composition strategy.
- Resupply lockers (see weapon-stats doc) are placed near secondary routes, not main chokepoints — encourages map awareness over camping the fight.
- Lore ties every map back to the central premise: these are the same contested sites, fought over and re-lost every quarter, because the loop is the point.

---

## MAP 1 — THE CRADLE

**Mode:** Frontier Push (sequential 3-point attack/defend)
**Setting:** An Aurelite extraction rig suspended above a fractured asteroid. Exposed gantries and cargo elevators connect the upper rig to the refinery interior below.

**Layout:**
1. **Docking Bay** (attacker spawn / first point) — open approach, long sightlines favor ranged defenders.
2. **Refinery Floor** (mid point) — tight interior, catwalks above, verticality favors flanking mobility classes.
3. **Extraction Core** (final point) — cramped interior chokepoint, ideal for a defensive hold.

**Callouts:** Docking Bay, Upper Gantry, Refinery Floor, Cargo Lift, Extraction Core.

**Tactical notes:**
- Outer gantries reward Vex and Wraith flanking routes.
- The Refinery Floor's tight geometry is a natural Bulwark/Forge choke.
- Cinder's Flare Wall is strong at Extraction Core's single entrance.
- Docking Bay's open sightlines are Reveille's best zone on the map.

**Lore:** The Cradle's Extraction Core is where the current quarter's quota gets decided. Whoever holds it when the contract timer ends keeps the site until the next cycle — which is to say, not for long.

## MAP 2 — HALCYON'S REACH

**Mode:** Chokepoint Control (single contested point, symmetrical)
**Setting:** An abandoned drift-station built around a hyperlane comms/nav array — the literal reason the war exists. Fully symmetrical layout with two flanking maintenance tunnels.

**Layout:**
- **Central Spire** (the point) — open, multi-tiered, sightlines from three directions.
- **East / West Causeway** — mirrored flank routes connecting spawns to the point.
- **Reactor Underlink** — basement flank route with low-gravity pockets that alter jump height and rocket-jump arcs.

**Callouts:** Central Spire, East Causeway, West Causeway, Reactor Underlink, Spire Catwalk.

**Tactical notes:**
- Perfect symmetry makes this the best map for testing 1v1 matchups from the counter matrix.
- Low-gravity Underlink sections change Marrow's rocket-jump distance and Vex's dash range — an advanced tech spot for experienced players.
- Central Spire's open sightlines favor Reveille but expose anyone holding the point too long.

**Lore:** Named after Vantage-Halcyon's original claim on the junction. Neither faction has held it for more than one full contract cycle in a row — the station has changed hands so many times the maintenance logs stopped tracking ownership.

## MAP 3 — DEAD RECKONING

**Mode:** Escort/Extraction (payload-style — one team escorts an autonomous cargo hauler, the other stops it)
**Setting:** The demilitarized checkpoint marking the tribunal's dead-zone boundary. A rail line runs an armored Aurelite hauler through checkpoint gates toward a jump gate.

**Layout:**
1. **Checkpoint Alpha** (payload start, defender spawn nearby)
2. **The Waiting Line** (mid — a corridor of abandoned civilian transports left at the checkpoint; narrow sightlines)
3. **Jump Gate Approach** (final stretch, wide open, exposed)

**Callouts:** Checkpoint Alpha, The Waiting Line, Transport Row, Jump Gate Approach.

**Tactical notes:**
- The hauler itself provides mobile cover for the escorting team.
- The Waiting Line's narrow corridor is prime real estate for Breach and Cinder area denial.
- Jump Gate Approach's open ground favors Reveille and Marrow on defense, but punishes overextension hard.
- Forge sentries near the payload track punish defenders who push too far forward to try to stop it early.

**Lore:** "Dead Reckoning" is contractor slang — nobody on either payroll is ever quite sure whose jurisdiction it falls under once the shooting starts, which is exactly how both corporations prefer it.
