# BLACKOUT CLAUSE
### A sci-fi mercenary hero shooter — Story & Character Bible

---

## PREMISE

Earth's fuel reserves ran dry two centuries ago. The only thing that kept humanity moving was **Aurelite** — a metastable isotope that makes faster-than-light travel possible, found in one place: the **Kepler Drift**, a shattered asteroid belt on the edge of colonized space.

Two megacorporations hold competing claims to the Drift — **Vantage-Halcyon** and the **Solari Combine**. An interstellar tribunal ruled that open war between them would collapse the FTL supply chain for everyone, so it banned direct conflict. What it couldn't ban was **the Blackout Clause** — a legal loophole letting each corporation hire a private military contractor to fight "sanctioned incursions" in designated dead zones. No flags, no formal declarations, no accountability. If it isn't official, it isn't a war.

So the corporations hire mercenaries. The same handful of contested rigs, checkpoints, and drift-stations get fought over, lost, and retaken every quarter, because the extraction quota shifts to whoever wins the ground that season. Nobody wins the war. Everyone just re-signs the contract.

That's the loop — and it's the reason a match-based shooter makes narrative sense: **the fighting is designed to never resolve.** The corporations need it that way. The mercs fight anyway, because contract debt, blacklists, and a collapsed civilian economy mean there's nowhere else to go.

---

## THE TWO FACTIONS

**Meridian Tactical Solutions** — Vantage-Halcyon's contractor. Uniform kit, numbered call signs, chain of command. They believe the contract is a code of honor, even if the people who wrote it have none. Cold blue-grey armor, clean lines.

**Obsidian Reach** — Solari Combine's contractor, though half the roster would tell you they work for themselves. Scavenged and modified gear, no two rigs alike. They think Meridian's discipline is a leash. Rust-red and black, welded plating, salvage aesthetic.

Both sides field the same nine roles — this halves your asset workload (reskin, not redesign) while keeping matches symmetric, same as TF2's RED/BLU structure.

## THE ROSTER (9)

**1. VEX — Recon / Mobility (Offense)**
Twin auto-pistols. Low HP, high speed.
- *Ability:* Blink Dash — short teleport dash; refunds cooldown on a kill within 3 seconds of landing.
- *Ultimate:* Adrenal Surge — brief fire-rate and move-speed spike.
- *Bio:* Ran smuggling routes through derelict stations as a kid. Fast because the slow ones didn't come home.

**2. MARROW — Assault (Offense, Burst Damage)**
Shoulder-fired rocket launcher, rocket-jump capable.
- *Ability:* Impact Jump — self-damage launch for verticality/escape.
- *Ultimate:* Barrage — three rapid-fire rockets.
- *Bio:* Cashiered officer, blacklisted from regular service after refusing to fire on striking miners. This is the only work left that doesn't ask questions.

**3. CINDER — Incendiary (Offense, Area Denial)**
Short-range plasma thrower; afterburn damage over time.
- *Ability:* Flare Wall — drops a burning barrier that blocks sightlines and damages on contact.
- *Ultimate:* Incinerate — large AoE burn pulse.
- *Bio:* Former reactor fire-response tech. Survived the blaze that killed her crew. Doesn't talk about it, doesn't need to — you can hear it when the flamethrower kicks on.

**4. BREACH — Demolitions (Offense, Utility)**
Grenade launcher + remote-detonated charges.
- *Ability:* Charge Plant — sticky charges, manually detonated; can be chained for a "breach-jump."
- *Ultimate:* Collapse — detonates every planted charge at once.
- *Bio:* Combat engineer who lost half his crew to a bad demo job. The cybernetic arm does the careful work his real one couldn't anymore.

**5. BULWARK — Heavy (Defense, Damage Soak)**
Chain-fed rotary cannon in a powered exo-frame. Slow, high HP.
- *Ability:* Kinetic Shield — deployable frontal energy barrier.
- *Ultimate:* Overdrive — massive fire-rate boost, movement locked.
- *Bio:* Conscripted as a child soldier by a corporate militia. Aged out into contract work because it's the only life on record. Watches out for the younger recruits without ever saying why.

**6. FORGE — Engineer (Defense, Structures)**
Light sidearm + nanite constructor tool.
- *Ability:* Deployable sentry turret and repair/build kit.
- *Ultimate:* Fortify — temporary overcharge on all deployed structures.
- *Bio:* Colony engineer whose settlement got quietly demolished for an Aurelite claim. Builds walls professionally now, because the ones that mattered didn't hold.

**7. SUTURE — Field Medic (Support, Healing)**
Nanite injector beam + stim-dart sidearm.
- *Ability:* Overcharge Stim — temporary damage resistance for an ally, with a crash penalty after.
- *Ultimate:* Mass Revive Pulse — brief team-wide burst heal.
- *Bio:* Lost her license treating both sides of a labor uprising. Tells herself treating whoever's paying isn't the same as not caring. Isn't sure she believes it.

**8. REVEILLE — Marksman (Offense, Precision)**
Charge rifle — hold to charge, release to fire.
- *Ability:* Recon Dart — briefly reveals enemies through walls in a radius.
- *Ultimate:* Railshot — fully charged shot that penetrates cover for guaranteed high damage.
- *Bio:* Last survivor of a colonial militia unit after a corporate "peacekeeping" op went wrong. Fights by a code the job keeps testing.

**9. WRAITH — Infiltrator (Offense, Deception)**
EMP blade (melee execute) + silenced sidearm.
- *Ability:* Active Camo — cloak, breaks on firing; can drop a holographic decoy.
- *Ultimate:* Ghost Protocol — guaranteed-execute stealth window.
- *Bio:* Had her identity wiped by the corp that trained her. Sells secrets and violence for a living, and isn't entirely sure what she'd be without either.

## DELIVERING THE STORY WITHOUT A FULL CAMPAIGN

Realistic for a solo/small-team portfolio build — narrative lives in the margins, not cutscenes:

- **Pre-match briefings**: a single voiced line per map from a faction handler ("Extraction window's ninety seconds. Don't get cute.")
- **Killfeed flavor lines**: character-specific one-liners on kills/deaths tied to their bio (Suture never taunts; Wraith never says anything at all)
- **In-universe patch notes**: balance changes framed as corporate memos or contractor bulletins — cheap to write, reinforces world
- **Environmental storytelling**: burned-out Meridian dropship on an Obsidian map, graffiti, contractor tallies scratched into walls
- **Loadout unlocks**: cosmetic gear tied to short bio-expanding blurbs instead of pure grind rewards
